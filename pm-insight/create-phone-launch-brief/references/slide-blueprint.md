# Slide Blueprint

## Default three-part structure

Start with about 12 slides; use 10–16 or the user-requested length. Preserve the three body sections in order and label each content slide with its Part. Use section labels rather than empty divider slides when space is tight.

| Pages (illustrative) | Section | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | 封面 | 《XX 手机新品发布会速递》, event date/market, information cutoff, edition and sourced hero visual |
| P2–P4 | Part 1 手机新品概览 | model and launch-price/configuration matrix; chips, displays, battery/design upgrades; camera lens hardware and documented predecessor differences |
| P5–P6 | Part 2 操作系统与系统软件升级 | OS/version overview, major system/software changes and source-described selling points, compatible devices and rollout |
| P7–P11 | Part 3 重点应用与功能特性 | AI, camera, gallery, desktop/lock screen and communications; one supported theme per page, combine or expand as evidence warrants |
| P12+ | 来源与更新记录 | full source index, video locators, coverage gaps, pending items and version record; add appendix pages for legibility |

Adjust page allocations to source richness. Keep Part 1 substantive even when hardware has no direct software dependency. Keep Part 2 at system level and Part 3 at task/feature level. Do not reuse the same content in both parts. Do not add HarmonyOS watchpoints or recommendations.

## Key-feature page recipe

Use a direct capability title, one primary sourced visual and a flat editorial composition. Include:

- A source-described scenario and concise entry/operation sequence when available.
- The official selling point or demonstrated result, or an explicitly attributed media observation.
- Material device, region, language, account, network, version and release constraints.
- Evidence label and claim-level source marker.

Do not fill a missing interaction or screenshot from memory. Use a sourced device image and supported editable text when no feature screenshot is available; label illustrative device imagery as such.

## Visual direction

- Use 16:9, derive the palette from event art or the supplied template, and prefer large sourced visuals with restrained text.
- Keep tables, price/configuration matrices and flows editable; preserve image aspect ratios.
- Use at least 42 pt for the deck title, 32 pt for page titles and 17 pt for body copy unless a template specifies otherwise. Split dense tables and source appendices instead of shrinking them excessively.
- Use concrete Chinese wording. Do not introduce unsupported headlines, marketing adjectives or conclusions.
- Keep citations secondary but readable. A source page alone does not replace claim-level attribution.

## Source and notes treatment

Put `[S#]` or `[S# HH:MM:SS]` beside material claims, table rows/cells and visuals. When a row mixes sources, identify which source supports each differing fact. Mark media material with the outlet and type, such as “×× 媒体实测”.

In each slide's notes record the full title, publisher, publication/access date, URL, region and paragraph/section/video locator for facts and images. Include full URLs in the source appendix. Record edition, generation time, added sources, corrections and coverage limits. Do not invent timestamps when only text was available.

## Acceptance checks

- Deliver an editable, rendered and visually checked PPTX.
- Preserve Part 1 → Part 2 → Part 3; allocate the most detailed coverage to Part 3 when evidence supports it.
- Include all in-scope phones and available launch prices/configurations, chips and lens hardware. Identify comparison baselines and missing specifications.
- Explain OS/system-software upgrades and rollout; distinguish earlier announcements and later updates.
- Review AI, camera, gallery, desktop and communications coverage without fabricating missing features.
- Support every claim, selling point, comparison and visual with official or qualified mainstream-media evidence; preserve source/test conditions and attribution.
- Exclude unsolicited HarmonyOS implications, PM action plans and unsupported interpretation.
- Distinguish announcement, demo, media report, hands-on test and availability.
- Include complete sources, available locators and material gaps; avoid overflow, overlap and distorted images.
